# VESTIGE — Vintage Design System

## 1. Aesthetic Identity

VESTIGE embodies the timeless craftsmanship of an **antique archive, a master technician's ledger, and a Victorian restoration workshop**. The design rejects flat, sterile modern SaaS tropes in favor of warm textures, engraved borders, and serif typography.

## 3. Typography Scale

- **Display & Titles:** `Playfair Display` (Serif, elegant high contrast)
- **Body & Controls:** `Libre Baskerville` (Legible classic serif)
- **Quotes & Embellishments:** `IM Fell English` (Italicized antique flourish)
- **Technical & Codes:** `Courier Prime` (Monospace typewriter feel)
